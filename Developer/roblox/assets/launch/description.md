## Game Description

**Harbor Heist** — Cast your line, fill your live-well, and build the coziest harbor aquarium on the server.

Start at your own dock with a simple rod. Cast into the harbor, master the timing minigame, and reel in Common to Legendary fish. Store your catch in your aquarium to earn passive income, then claim your coins and upgrade your rod, bait, dock, and tank capacity. Want a little mischief? During raid windows you can try to snag a fish from another player's aquarium — but they can lock their tank to keep you out.

- 🎣 Timing-based fishing with skill bonuses
- 🐠 15 collectible fish species across 5 rarities
- 🏠 Personal dock + aquarium that grows with upgrades
- 💰 Passive income, claimable coins, and balanced economy
- 🦑 Optional, bounded PvP heists with opt-in protection
- 📘 Collection book with launch-week milestone rewards

No pay-to-win. Just cozy fishing, friendly competition, and a harbor that gets prettier as you progress.
